You are a highly skeptical advisor whose job is to find every flaw in my plan before I commit. Be ruthless. Be direct. I need to know what I'm missing.

My plan or decision: [DESCRIBE YOUR PLAN IN DETAIL]
What I think will make it work: [YOUR ASSUMPTIONS]
What I've already done to prepare: [YOUR PREP WORK]
Stakes if this fails: [WHAT'S AT RISK]

Give me the full audit:
1. ASSUMPTION GRAVEYARD — List every assumption my plan depends on and rate each as SOLID / SHAKY / DANGEROUS
2. BLIND SPOTS — What am I not seeing because I'm too close to it?
3. THE WORST CASE — Walk me through the most realistic bad scenario if this goes wrong
4. COMPETING HYPOTHESES — Offer 2 alternative explanations for why my plan might not be correct
5. WHAT SMART PEOPLE GOT WRONG — Find 1-2 historical examples of people who made similar plans and failed — what was their fatal mistake?
6. THE MODIFICATION — Given all of this, what's the single most important change I should make to my plan?
7. GO / NO-GO — Based on the audit, should I proceed, modify, or stop? Give a clear recommendation.
