Help me find a side project that sits at the magical intersection of three things: skills I already have, topics I genuinely find interesting, and problems people would actually pay to solve.

My inventory:
- Skills I'm good at: [LIST YOUR TOP 5 SKILLS]
- Skills I'm learning: [WHAT YOU'RE DEVELOPING]
- Topics I could talk about for hours: [YOUR GENUINE INTERESTS]
- Problems I've personally experienced: [FRUSTRATIONS YOU'VE HAD]
- Tools I know how to use: [SOFTWARE, PLATFORMS, ETC.]
- Time available per week: [BE HONEST]

Generate:
1. THE VENN DIAGRAM: Map my skills × interests × real problems and find 5 overlap zones
2. THE TOP 10 IDEAS: Ranked by feasibility × potential × personal enjoyment
3. THE MVP FOR EACH: What's the smallest version I could build in a weekend?
4. THE VALIDATION TEST: For each idea, one thing I could do in 30 minutes to check if anyone actually wants this
5. THE UNFAIR ADVANTAGE: Which idea would I have a unique edge in that most people wouldn't?
6. THE RECOMMENDATION: Pick the ONE idea I should start this week, and tell me why

Don't give me billion-dollar startup ideas. Give me things I can actually build that might make $1,000/month.
