Counterintuitively, giving AI MORE freedom produces WORSE results. Strategic constraints force better thinking. Teach me this technique through demonstration.

My task: [WHAT YOU WANT AI TO HELP WITH]

Demonstrate the constraint injection method:
1. UNCONSTRAINED: Do the task with no special instructions (show the mediocre default)
2. LENGTH CONSTRAINT: Now do it in exactly 100 words (notice how precision increases)
3. AUDIENCE CONSTRAINT: Now do it for a specific person who would be very hard to impress: [CHOOSE: a skeptical investor / a brilliant 12-year-old / your harshest critic]
4. FORMAT CONSTRAINT: Now do it as [CHOOSE: a numbered list of exactly 7 items / a dialogue between two people / a decision tree]
5. EXCLUSION CONSTRAINT: Now do it without using any of these words: [LIST 5 COMMON WORDS IN YOUR DOMAIN]
6. COMBINATION: Stack 3 of the above constraints simultaneously

Then reveal:
- Why each constraint improved the output (the cognitive mechanism)
- My personal CONSTRAINT MENU: 15 constraints I can mix and match for any future prompt
- THE RULE OF THREE: Why exactly 3 stacked constraints usually hits the sweet spot
