The most creative solutions come from extreme constraints. Let's weaponize yours.

My challenge or goal: [YOUR GOAL]
My biggest constraints: [LIST YOUR REAL LIMITATIONS]
Resources I don't have: [TIME/MONEY/TEAM/SKILLS]

Using constraint-driven creativity:
1. Pick the most limiting constraint and make it a design principle
2. Find 3 examples where this exact constraint led to a better outcome
3. Redesign your approach AS IF this constraint were the product's selling point
4. What could you accomplish if you had only 10% of the resources you think you need?
5. What would a broke genius do in your situation?
6. Create the version of this that costs nothing and takes 3 days to test

The output should feel uncomfortable — like it might actually work.
