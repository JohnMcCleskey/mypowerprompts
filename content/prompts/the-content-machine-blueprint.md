I have one idea (or experience or insight) and I want to turn it into a month's worth of content across multiple platforms without it feeling repetitive. This is how prolific creators actually work.

My one idea/insight:
[DESCRIBE YOUR CORE IDEA, LESSON, OR EXPERIENCE]

Explode it into a content ecosystem:
1. THE LONG-FORM ANCHOR: One 1500-word blog post / newsletter that fully explores the idea
2. THE THREAD: A 10-tweet thread that distills the key insights
3. THE HOOK VARIATIONS: 5 different opening hooks for the same idea (curiosity, controversy, story, data, question)
4. THE STORY VERSION: The idea told as a personal narrative
5. THE FRAMEWORK VERSION: The idea structured as a 3-5 step framework
6. THE CONTRARIAN VERSION: The idea presented as a challenge to conventional wisdom
7. THE VISUAL CONCEPT: Describe a single image or infographic that captures the idea
8. THE VIDEO SCRIPT: A 60-second script for a talking-head video
9. THE NEWSLETTER SNIPPET: A 200-word version for an email
10. THE COMMENT BAIT: 3 questions I can ask audiences that relate to this idea and generate discussion

Show me how each piece feels different while reinforcing the same core message.
