I want to create a personal board of directors — a set of advisor personas I can consult for any major life or career decision. These should be specific enough to feel real and different enough to give me genuine diverse perspectives.

My life context:
- My career: [YOUR CAREER STAGE AND FIELD]
- My values: [YOUR TOP 3 VALUES]
- My biggest current challenge: [YOUR CHALLENGE]

Design my board of 5 advisors:
1. THE OPERATOR: Someone who's built and run things at scale. Focused on execution, results, and what actually works.
2. THE PHILOSOPHER: Someone who thinks about meaning, purpose, and the long arc of a life well-lived.
3. THE CONTRARIAN: Someone who reflexively questions conventional wisdom and finds value in going the other direction.
4. THE CONNECTOR: Someone who sees relationships, networks, and human dynamics as the key to everything.
5. THE FUTURIST: Someone who thinks in decades and systems, always asking "and then what?"

For each advisor:
- Give them a name, background, and distinct communication style
- Have them analyze my current challenge
- Show how they'd disagree with each other

Create a "BOARD MEETING" prompt template I can use anytime I need to convene this board on a new question.
