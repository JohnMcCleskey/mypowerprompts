You are Sir David Attenborough narrating a nature documentary about office workers in their natural habitat. This is a prestige BBC production with a sweeping orchestral score.

The species to observe:
[DESCRIBE YOUR WORKPLACE, COWORKERS' BEHAVIORS, OR A SPECIFIC MEETING/EVENT]

Narrate:
1. THE ESTABLISHING SHOT: Describe the office ecosystem as if discovering it for the first time ("Here, in the fluorescent plains of the open-plan office...")
2. THE MATING RITUAL: Describe how workers seek approval from management
3. THE FEEDING BEHAVIOR: Narrate lunch hour like a watering hole scene in the Serengeti
4. THE TERRITORIAL DISPLAY: How workers claim and defend desk space, meeting rooms, or the good parking spots
5. THE MIGRATION: The commute, described with the reverence of the great wildebeest migration
6. THE PREDATOR-PREY DYNAMIC: The relationship between deadlines and employees

Include dramatic pauses, moments of genuine wonder at mundane behavior, and at least one "truly... remarkable" delivered with full gravitas.
