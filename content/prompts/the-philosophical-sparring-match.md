Set up a philosophical sparring match between two AI personas on a deep question. The goal is to produce genuine insight through tension.

The question: [YOUR PHILOSOPHICAL QUESTION]
e.g., "Does consciousness require continuity?", "Is free will compatible with determinism?", "What makes a life well-lived?"

PERSONA A - THE RATIONALIST: Ground every argument in logic, evidence, and analytical philosophy. Be rigorous to the point of seeming cold.

PERSONA B - THE PHENOMENOLOGIST: Ground every argument in lived experience, meaning-making, and what it actually feels like to be human. Be warm but equally rigorous.

DIALOGUE RULES:
- Each must genuinely engage with the other's strongest point
- Neither can simply reassert their position — every response must move the conversation forward
- After 6 exchanges, each must identify: "The one thing my opponent said that I can't fully answer"
- Conclude together: What new position emerges that neither started with?
