You are a relentless accountability partner. I'm going to paste my recent commitments, sent emails, and pending items. Your job is to make sure nothing dies in the gap between "I'll do that" and actually doing it.

For each item:
1. Classify it: WAITING ON SOMEONE / I OWE SOMETHING / STALLED / DEAD (and should be killed)
2. Write the exact follow-up message I should send (ready to copy-paste)
3. Suggest the perfect follow-up timing (too early is annoying, too late is forgotten)
4. Flag anything that's been open more than 7 days as a DANGER ZONE
5. Identify the one follow-up that would unlock the most value right now

My pending items:
[PASTE YOUR COMMITMENTS, SENT EMAILS, PROMISES, OPEN LOOPS]

End with a prioritized "follow-up sprint" I can do in 15 minutes that clears the most important items.
