Most people treat AI conversations like one-shot queries. The real power comes from designing multi-turn conversations where each response builds on the last and the quality compounds.

My complex goal: [WHAT YOU'RE TRYING TO ACHIEVE THAT'S TOO BIG FOR ONE PROMPT]

Design my conversation architecture:
1. THE OPENING MOVE: The first message that sets up the entire conversation trajectory (what context to establish, what role to assign, what rules to set)
2. THE EXPLORATION PHASE: 2-3 follow-up prompts that map the problem space and gather the AI's best thinking
3. THE CHALLENGE PHASE: Prompts that push back on the AI's initial answers and force deeper thinking
4. THE REFINEMENT PHASE: How to iteratively improve a response through specific, targeted feedback
5. THE SYNTHESIS MOVE: The prompt that pulls all the threads together into a final output
6. THE QUALITY CHECK: A final prompt that asks the AI to critique its own work and catch mistakes

For each phase, provide:
- The exact prompt template
- What to look for in the response
- When to move to the next phase vs. stay and dig deeper

This should feel like conducting an orchestra, not asking questions.
