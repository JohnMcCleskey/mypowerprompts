There are things I know I don't know (known unknowns). But the most dangerous gaps are the things I don't even know I'm missing (unknown unknowns). Help me map my knowledge gaps.

My domain/field: [YOUR AREA OF WORK OR INTEREST]
My experience level: [YEARS / CONTEXT]
What I think I know well: [YOUR PERCEIVED STRENGTHS]
What I know I don't know: [YOUR ACKNOWLEDGED GAPS]

Map my knowledge landscape:
1. THE KNOWN UNKNOWNS: Validate my self-assessment — are my acknowledged gaps actually where they should be?
2. THE UNKNOWN UNKNOWNS: Identify 5 areas I probably don't realize I'm missing:
   - What adjacent fields would an expert say I should know about?
   - What foundational concepts might I have wrong without knowing it?
   - What has changed recently that invalidated something I learned?
3. THE DANGEROUS GAPS: Which gaps could actually hurt me if they stay unfilled?
4. THE LEARNING PRIORITY MATRIX: Rank all identified gaps by: IMPACT × FILLABILITY
5. THE 30-DAY PLAN: For the top 3 gaps, suggest specific resources and a learning timeline
6. THE HUMILITY MAP: A visualization of my domain showing "what I know," "what I know I don't know," and "what I didn't realize I was missing"

This should feel slightly humbling. That's the point.
