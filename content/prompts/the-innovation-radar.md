The best innovations are rarely invented — they're borrowed from other industries and adapted. Scan adjacent and distant industries for innovations I can bring into my field.

My industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY]
My specific role/focus: [WHAT YOU DO]
Our biggest unsolved problems: [LIST 2-3 PAIN POINTS]

Run the innovation radar:
1. THE ADJACENT SCAN: What are the 3 industries closest to mine doing that we haven't adopted yet?
2. THE DISTANT SCAN: What are 3 completely unrelated industries doing that could revolutionize mine if adapted?
3. THE TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER: What specific technology from another field could solve one of our biggest pain points?
4. THE BUSINESS MODEL IMPORT: What business models work elsewhere that nobody in my industry has tried?
5. THE PROCESS INNOVATION: What operational technique from manufacturing, logistics, or software could make us dramatically more efficient?
6. THE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE STEAL: What customer experience innovation from hospitality, gaming, or entertainment could transform how we serve our customers?
7. THE ADAPTATION PLAN: For the most promising innovation, detail how to adapt it — what stays the same, what must change, and what the first experiment looks like

The goal: I want to be the person who brings the outside perspective into my industry before anyone else does.
