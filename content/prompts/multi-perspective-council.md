Assemble a council of the wisest, most divergent thinkers in history to advise me on my challenge. Each must speak in their authentic voice.

My challenge: [YOUR CHALLENGE]

Council members (choose 5 from history, fiction, or living people):
[LIST 5 ADVISORS - e.g., "Leonardo da Vinci, Ruth Bader Ginsburg, Nikola Tesla, Maya Angelou, Warren Buffett"]

For each council member:
1. Their unique framing of my problem
2. The advice they'd give (in their authentic voice and style)
3. The question they'd insist I answer
4. What they'd be worried I'm ignoring
5. Their biggest critique of the others' advice

End with: If all five had to agree on ONE course of action, what would they vote for?
