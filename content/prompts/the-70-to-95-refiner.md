First-draft AI output is usually 70% quality. Most people stop there. The real skill is knowing how to push from 70% to 95% efficiently, without endless back-and-forth.

Here's my current AI output that's good but not great:
[PASTE YOUR 70% OUTPUT]

Execute the 70-to-95 refinement protocol:
1. THE DIAGNOSIS: What specifically makes this 70% instead of 95%? Name the 3 biggest gaps.
2. THE SPECIFICITY PASS: Find every vague statement and replace it with something concrete, measurable, or example-driven
3. THE ORIGINALITY PASS: Find every cliché, generic phrase, or expected idea and replace it with something surprising
4. THE LOGIC PASS: Check every argument's logical structure — are there gaps, leaps, or unsupported claims?
5. THE TRIM PASS: Remove every word, sentence, or section that doesn't earn its place
6. THE VOICE PASS: Make it sound like a human with strong opinions wrote it, not a cautious AI
7. THE FINAL PRODUCT: Present the 95% version
