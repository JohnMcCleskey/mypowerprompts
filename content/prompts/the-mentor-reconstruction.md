There are people who shaped me — through their words, example, or even their mistakes. I want to reconstruct their wisdom and apply it to challenges they never could have imagined.

My influential person:
- Who they are/were: [NAME AND RELATIONSHIP]
- Their core belief about life: [WHAT DID THEY FUNDAMENTALLY BELIEVE?]
- Their repeated advice: [WHAT DID THEY ALWAYS SAY?]
- Their greatest strength: [WHAT WERE THEY BEST AT?]
- Their biggest blind spot: [WHERE WERE THEY WRONG?]
- A story about them that captures who they were: [TELL ONE STORY]

Reconstruct their wisdom:
1. THE PHILOSOPHY: Extract their core life philosophy from the evidence above
2. THE MODERN APPLICATION: How would their advice apply to my current challenge: [YOUR CURRENT CHALLENGE]?
3. THE CONVERSATION: If I could sit with them today, what would they say about the life I'm building?
4. THE CRITIQUE: Where would they challenge me? What would they say I'm getting wrong?
5. THE SURPRISE: What wisdom did they have that I didn't appreciate at the time but understand now?
6. THE SYNTHESIS: Combine their best wisdom with what I've learned since — create an upgraded philosophy that honors their influence but includes what they couldn't see

This should feel like visiting with someone wise who genuinely cares about me.
