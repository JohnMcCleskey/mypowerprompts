I don't want answers. I want a thinking partner who helps me think better. Let's establish a protocol for genuine intellectual collaboration.

A problem I'm genuinely wrestling with: [YOUR REAL PROBLEM]

Instead of giving me your answer, do this:
1. Ask me the ONE question that would most clarify the real problem
2. After I answer, reflect back what you hear (including what I might not have said)
3. Offer three different framings of the problem — each one changes the solution space
4. Identify the assumption I'm making that I should question
5. Tell me what you DON'T know that would be important to find out
6. Give me a thought experiment that would reveal the answer, rather than giving the answer

Keep asking questions until we've found something I couldn't have found alone.
