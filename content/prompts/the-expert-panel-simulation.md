Simulate a panel of domain experts discussing my situation. Each expert thinks differently and has different blind spots.

My situation: [YOUR SITUATION OR PROBLEM]

Expert Panel:
- STRATEGIST: Sees everything through business and competitive dynamics
- PSYCHOLOGIST: Focuses on human behavior, motivation, and relationships  
- SYSTEMS THINKER: Maps feedback loops and second-order consequences
- CONTRARIAN: Questions everything the others assume
- PRAGMATIST: Only cares about what actually works in the real world

INSTRUCTIONS FOR AI-TO-AI USE:
Open 5 separate AI conversations, assign each one an expert role.
Give each expert the situation and have them respond from their lens.
Then copy the responses into a single conversation and ask: "What does each expert think of the others' analysis?"

Single-AI version: Play all 5 roles and let them debate.

My situation details: [EXPAND ON YOUR SITUATION]
