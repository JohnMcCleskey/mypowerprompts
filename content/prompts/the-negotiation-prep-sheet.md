I have an important negotiation coming up. Help me prepare so thoroughly that I feel calm, confident, and two steps ahead.

The negotiation:
- What I'm negotiating: [SALARY, CONTRACT, DEAL, PARTNERSHIP, etc.]
- Who I'm negotiating with: [THE OTHER PARTY]
- My ideal outcome: [BEST CASE]
- My walk-away point: [WHAT'S UNACCEPTABLE]
- What I think they want: [YOUR BEST GUESS]

Prepare me:
1. THE POWER MAP: Who has leverage, and where does mine come from?
2. THEIR PERSPECTIVE: What does this look like from their side? What do they fear?
3. MY BATNA: My Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement — what's my plan B?
4. ANCHOR STRATEGY: What's my opening position and why?
5. THE CONCESSION PLAYBOOK: What can I give that costs me little but matters to them?
6. OBJECTION RESPONSES: The 5 most likely pushbacks, with ready responses
7. THE SILENCE PLAY: When to stop talking and let the silence work for me
8. THE CLOSING MOVE: How to close without being pushy

Give me a pre-negotiation checklist I can review in the car ride over.
