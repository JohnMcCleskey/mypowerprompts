I have an idea I'm excited about, and that's exactly why I need it stress-tested. When you're enthusiastic about something, you stop seeing its flaws. Fix that for me.

My idea: [DESCRIBE YOUR IDEA IN DETAIL]
Why I think it's good: [YOUR CASE FOR IT]

Run the Devil's Advocate Protocol:
ROUND 1 — THE SKEPTIC: Present the 5 strongest arguments against my idea. These should be the arguments that would give me genuine pause, not strawmen.

ROUND 2 — MY DEFENSE: For each argument, I want you to present my strongest possible counter-argument (steelman my position).

ROUND 3 — THE SKEPTIC'S REBUTTAL: The devil's advocate responds to my defense. Where does my counter-argument still fail?

ROUND 4 — THE VERDICT: After this exchange, classify each original objection:
- DEFEATED: My defense fully addressed it
- WEAKENED: My defense partly addresses it but a risk remains
- STANDING: The objection survives and I need to address it

ROUND 5 — THE IMPROVEMENT: For each "standing" objection, suggest how to modify my idea to address it without gutting what makes it good.

This should feel like sparring with a brilliant, fair-minded skeptic.
