I want to get measurably better at using AI over time. That requires tracking what works, what doesn't, and why. Help me set up an AI usage audit system.

Build my audit trail:
1. THE SESSION LOG TEMPLATE: A quick (under 60 seconds) post-session template to capture:
   - Task type
   - Prompt approach used
   - Quality of output (1-5)
   - What worked / what didn't
   - Time saved vs. doing it manually

2. THE WEEKLY REVIEW: Questions to ask myself weekly about my AI usage patterns:
   - What type of task produces the best results?
   - What type consistently disappoints?
   - Am I getting better at prompting? Evidence?
   - Where am I still defaulting to manual when AI could help?

3. THE PROMPT JOURNAL: A system for saving and categorizing my best prompts for reuse

4. THE ANTI-PATTERNS: Common mistakes to watch for as I use AI more:
   - Over-reliance on AI for tasks I should think through
   - Under-utilizing AI for tasks I'm doing manually
   - Accepting first-draft quality when I should iterate

5. THE MONTHLY METRICS: 3 numbers to track monthly that show whether my AI usage is actually saving time and improving quality

6. THE EVOLUTION PLAN: How to deliberately upgrade my AI skills every quarter

Make this lightweight enough that I'll actually do it.
