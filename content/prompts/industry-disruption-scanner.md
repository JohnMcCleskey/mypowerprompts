Analyze my industry through the lens of technological and social disruption. Don't give me obvious answers — help me see what I might be missing.

My industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY]
My role in it: [YOUR ROLE]
What I think the biggest trend is: [YOUR CURRENT VIEW]

Challenge my thinking:
1. What are the 3 most underrated shifts happening in my industry right now?
2. Which player nobody is watching is positioned to win big?
3. What assumption the entire industry shares that is probably wrong?
4. Which technology from a completely different field will unexpectedly transform mine?
5. What will my job look like in 5 years vs. today?
6. What should I be learning RIGHT NOW to stay ahead?

Prioritize insights that feel slightly uncomfortable — that's where the signal usually is.
