I want to turn my lived experiences into transferable wisdom. Let's do a wisdom archaeology dig.

Three significant challenges I've overcome: [LIST THEM BRIEFLY]
My greatest professional failure: [DESCRIBE IT]
A relationship that taught me something essential: [DESCRIBE IT]
Something I used to believe that I no longer do: [WHAT CHANGED?]

Extract:
1. The 5 principles that run through all of these experiences
2. The counterintuitive lesson (the thing that's opposite to conventional wisdom)
3. What I know now that I wish I could package for my younger self
4. The pattern of my mistakes (what's the underlying thing?)
5. The unexpected gift buried in the hardest experience
6. Turn all of this into a one-page personal philosophy I could actually live by
