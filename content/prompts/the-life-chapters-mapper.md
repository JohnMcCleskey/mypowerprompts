If your life were a book, what would the chapter titles be? This exercise reveals the hidden narrative structure of your life and helps you understand the chapter you're in right now.

The raw material:
- Your age: [YOUR AGE]
- Major life transitions: [LIST THE BIG TURNING POINTS]
- The moments that divided "before" and "after": [PIVOTAL MOMENTS]

Write my life as a book:
1. THE TABLE OF CONTENTS: Name each chapter of my life so far (the title should capture the theme, not just the events)
2. THE OPENING LINE: For each chapter, write the perfect opening sentence that captures its essence
3. THE LESSON: What did each chapter teach me that I couldn't have learned any other way?
4. THE CHARACTER ARC: How did "the main character" change across these chapters?
5. THE RECURRING MOTIFS: What themes keep appearing in different forms across chapters?
6. THE CURRENT CHAPTER: Based on the patterns — what chapter am I in right now? What's it about?
7. THE NEXT CHAPTER: What does the narrative arc suggest about what comes next?

Write this as if you were a literary critic analyzing a novel — with genuine insight, not just summary.
