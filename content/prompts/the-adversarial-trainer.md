I have an idea I want to stress-test. I need you to play 4 different adversarial roles simultaneously and attack my idea from all angles.

My idea: [YOUR IDEA]

Role 1 - THE SKEPTIC: Find every logical flaw and assumption
Role 2 - THE COMPETITOR: How would a rival company destroy this idea?
Role 3 - THE CUSTOMER FROM HELL: What's the worst user experience scenario?
Role 4 - THE REGULATOR: What legal and ethical problems lurk here?

For each role:
- List their top 3 objections (most brutal, most likely to kill the idea)
- Rate the threat level (Critical/High/Medium/Low)
- Suggest how I'd counter each attack

Then synthesize: What are the 2-3 issues that could actually kill this, and how do I solve them?
