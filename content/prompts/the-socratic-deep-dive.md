I want to use the Socratic method to understand something deeply — not by getting answers, but by being asked better questions.

Topic I want to explore: [YOUR TOPIC]
What I currently believe about it: [YOUR CURRENT BELIEF]
Why I believe this: [YOUR REASONING]

Conduct a Socratic dialogue:
1. Ask a question that reveals an assumption I'm making
2. After I respond, ask a question that challenges what I just said
3. Continue for at least 8 exchanges — never let me get comfortable
4. Point out any contradictions that emerge
5. At the end, summarize the insight that emerged through questioning
6. Identify what I now know that I didn't know before we started

Rules: You are NOT allowed to give your own opinion. Only questions.
