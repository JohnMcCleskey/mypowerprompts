I have a complex decision to make and I need more than gut feeling. Build me a comprehensive decision framework tailored to this specific choice.

My decision: [DESCRIBE THE DECISION]
Options I'm considering: [LIST YOUR OPTIONS]
What makes this hard: [WHY YOU'RE STUCK]
Timeline: [WHEN DO I NEED TO DECIDE?]

Build my decision matrix:
1. CRITERIA EXTRACTION: Identify the 6-8 factors that actually matter for this decision (not what "should" matter — what actually does)
2. WEIGHT ASSIGNMENT: Help me rank these criteria by true importance (use forced-rank pairwise comparison)
3. THE MATRIX: Score each option against each criterion (1-10 scale with specific anchors)
4. THE SENSITIVITY TEST: Which criterion, if I changed its weight, would flip my answer?
5. THE REGRET MINIMIZATION FRAMEWORK: In 10 years, which choice would I regret NOT making?
6. THE GUT CHECK: After all this analysis, does the answer feel right? If not, what's my gut seeing that the matrix isn't capturing?
7. THE COMMITMENT DEVICE: Once decided, what action can I take in the next 24 hours that makes the decision irreversible?

Show me the math, then tell me what the math can't tell me.
