Take this completely mundane moment from my day and retell it as if it were a critically acclaimed literary novel, an action movie, AND a Shakespearean tragedy. 

The mundane moment: [DESCRIBE SOMETHING BORING THAT HAPPENED]

For each format:
- Set the scene dramatically
- Give the main character (me) a grandiose internal monologue
- Build unbearable tension
- Create a climax worthy of awards
- End with a profound lesson learned

The more boring the original event, the better this works.
