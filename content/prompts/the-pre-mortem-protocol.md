We're going to run a pre-mortem on my project. This is the opposite of a post-mortem — instead of analyzing why something failed AFTER the fact, we'll imagine it's 6 months from now and the project has completely failed, then work backward to find out why.

My project/initiative:
[DESCRIBE YOUR PROJECT, GOALS, AND CURRENT PLAN]

Run the pre-mortem:
1. THE OBITUARY: It's 6 months from now and this project is dead. Write the story of how it died. Be specific and realistic.
2. THE USUAL SUSPECTS: What are the 5 most likely causes of failure? Rank by probability.
3. THE BLIND SPOTS: What am I not thinking about because I'm too close to this?
4. THE HUMAN FACTORS: Where will people (including me) be the weakest link?
5. THE EXTERNAL SHOCKS: What could change in the world that would kill this?
6. THE EARLY WARNING SYSTEM: For each failure mode, what's the earliest signal that it's happening?
7. THE PREVENTION PLAN: For the top 3 risks, what can I do THIS WEEK to reduce the probability?
8. THE KILL CRITERIA: Under what conditions should I abandon this project rather than keep going?

Be uncomfortable. I need honesty, not encouragement.
