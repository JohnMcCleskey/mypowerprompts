AI tends toward the average — the most common, most expected response. I want to push past that into genuinely creative territory. Teach me the techniques that unlock real originality.

First, demonstrate the difference. Take this topic:
[YOUR TOPIC OR CREATIVE CHALLENGE]

Generate:
1. THE GENERIC RESPONSE: What 90% of AI users would get with a simple prompt on this topic (show me the "default")
2. THE CONSTRAINT FLIP: Now add a constraint that forces unexpected thinking (e.g., "explain this using only questions" or "make the weakness the central feature")
3. THE PERSONA INJECTION: Have a specific unusual persona approach this (not "a poet" — more like "a burnt-out NASA engineer who became a kindergarten teacher")
4. THE RANDOM COLLISION: Combine this topic with something completely unrelated and find the surprising connection
5. THE ANTI-PATTERN: Do the opposite of what's expected and find the insight in the inversion
6. THE SYNTHESIS: Combine the best elements from approaches 2-5 into something none of them could produce alone

Then give me a CREATIVITY PROMPT TOOLKIT — 10 modifiers I can add to any prompt to push it past generic. Each one should be a single sentence I can append.
