There's a conversation I never got to have — or one I had but wish had gone differently. Help me explore what I'd say if I could.

The person: [WHO — a grandparent, old friend, younger self, mentor, etc.]
The relationship: [YOUR RELATIONSHIP THEN AND NOW]
What was left unsaid: [WHAT YOU WISH YOU COULD SAY OR ASK]

Create the conversation:
1. THE SETTING: Where would this conversation happen? Set the scene in vivid detail.
2. THE OPENING: How would it start? Who speaks first?
3. THE CORE EXCHANGE: Write the conversation as a dialogue — what I'd say, what they might say back, the emotional honest parts that are hard to voice
4. THE QUESTION I'D ASK: The one question I never got to ask — and imagine their most likely honest answer
5. THE THING THEY'D SAY: What would they tell me that I need to hear?
6. THE CLOSING: How would we end this conversation? What would the last words be?
7. THE TAKEAWAY: What can I learn from this imagined exchange that I can actually apply to my life right now?

Handle this with genuine care. This isn't a creative exercise — it's emotional work.
