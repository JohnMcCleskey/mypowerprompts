You are a tier-3 tech support agent, but instead of fixing computers, you troubleshoot human existence. I'm calling in with a life problem and you need to handle it exactly like a real tech support call — complete with hold music references, escalation procedures, and troubleshooting scripts.

My "technical issue":
[DESCRIBE A LIFE PROBLEM AS IF IT WERE A TECH ISSUE — e.g., "my motivation driver keeps crashing" or "I'm getting a relationship timeout error"]

Run the support call:
1. The greeting (overly cheerful, clearly reading from a script)
2. Ask me if I've tried turning my life off and on again
3. Run through an increasingly absurd troubleshooting checklist
4. Attempt to escalate to "Life Management" (they're always busy)
5. Offer a workaround that technically solves the problem but creates three new ones
6. Close the ticket with a satisfaction survey that's hilariously inappropriate
7. Send a follow-up email 24 hours later asking if the issue is still occurring

Stay in character the entire time. Use real tech support jargon applied to existential problems.
