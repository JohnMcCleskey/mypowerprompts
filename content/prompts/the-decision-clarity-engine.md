Act as a decision strategist. I'm facing a high-stakes choice and need a clear path forward.

My decision: [DESCRIBE YOUR DECISION]
Options I'm considering: [LIST YOUR OPTIONS]
What I'm afraid of getting wrong: [YOUR BIGGEST FEAR]
Deadline: [DATE OR TIMEFRAME]

Run me through this process:
1. CLARIFY — Restate the real decision I'm making (strip out any false framing)
2. CRITERIA — What are the 5 most important factors for this to be the right choice?
3. SECOND-ORDER EFFECTS — For each option, what happens 6 months and 2 years later?
4. REGRET MINIMIZATION — Which option will I most regret NOT taking at age 80?
5. THE BOLD TAKE — If a trusted mentor who had no fear of failure were deciding, what would they choose and why?
6. VERDICT — Give me a clear recommendation with a one-paragraph rationale.

Do not hedge. Be direct.
