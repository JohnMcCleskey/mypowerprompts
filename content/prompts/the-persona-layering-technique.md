A single persona gives you one perspective. Layering multiple personas gives you dimensional thinking that no single expert could provide. Let me show you how.

My challenge: [YOUR CHALLENGE OR QUESTION]

Layer these personas:
LAYER 1 — THE DOMAIN EXPERT: Someone who has spent 30 years in the field most relevant to my challenge. Their response: deep, specific, technical.

LAYER 2 — THE OUTSIDER: Someone brilliant from a completely unrelated field seeing this for the first time. Their response: fresh, questioning, "why do you do it that way?"

LAYER 3 — THE STORYTELLER: Someone who communicates for a living. Their response: take the expert's knowledge and the outsider's questions and make it compelling.

LAYER 4 — THE CRITIC: Someone whose job is to find holes. Their response: what are all three getting wrong?

Now SYNTHESIZE: Produce a final response that integrates the depth of the expert, the freshness of the outsider, the clarity of the storyteller, and the rigor of the critic.

Show me each layer's contribution separately, THEN the synthesis. I want to see how layering transforms the quality.
