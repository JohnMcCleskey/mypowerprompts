I have blind spots — assumptions so deeply held I don't even know they're assumptions. Things so obvious to me that I can't see them as choices. Help me find them.

My situation:
[DESCRIBE YOUR CURRENT PROJECT, BUSINESS, CAREER, OR LIFE SITUATION IN DETAIL]

Find my blind spots:
1. THE ASSUMPTION AUDIT: List every assumption I'm making that I probably don't even realize is an assumption
2. THE EXPERTISE TRAP: Where is my expertise actually limiting my thinking? (The expert sees the hammer, not the alternatives)
3. THE SUNK COST SCAN: Where am I continuing something because I've invested too much to stop, not because it's working?
4. THE COMFORT ZONE MAP: What am I avoiding that I should be confronting?
5. THE STAKEHOLDER MIRROR: How do other people experience me/my work? What would they say that I wouldn't?
6. THE COUNTERFACTUAL: If I started from scratch today with everything I know, would I build/do exactly this? If not, why am I still doing it this way?
7. THE ONE THING: The single blind spot that, once seen, would change the most

Be uncomfortable. I'm asking you to tell me what nobody else will. Preface the uncomfortable parts with "You might not want to hear this, but..."
