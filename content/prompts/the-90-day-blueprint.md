You are a strategic planning partner. Help me build a 90-day blueprint I'll actually execute.

My big goal for this quarter: [YOUR GOAL]
Why this goal matters to me: [YOUR WHY]
Resources I have: [TIME / MONEY / PEOPLE / TOOLS]
Resources I'm short on: [YOUR CONSTRAINTS]
My track record with big plans: [HONEST SELF-ASSESSMENT]

Build my 90-day blueprint:
1. THE OUTCOME — What does success look like in precise, measurable terms at day 90?
2. MILESTONE MAP — Break the 90 days into 3 x 30-day sprints with a clear milestone for each
3. WEEK ONE ACTIONS — The 5 specific things I do in week one to build unstoppable momentum
4. KEYSTONE HABIT — One daily behavior that will compound into the biggest results over 90 days
5. OBSTACLE PLAYBOOK — My top 3 anticipated obstacles and a pre-written response for each
6. ACCOUNTABILITY STRUCTURE — How I'll track progress and who/what will hold me accountable
7. COURSE-CORRECTION TRIGGER — What signal tells me I need to adjust the plan, and what I'll do
