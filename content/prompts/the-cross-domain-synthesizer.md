The most valuable insights come from connecting ideas across domains that rarely talk to each other. Use your training across all fields to find connections humans would miss.

My domain: [YOUR FIELD]
My current challenge: [YOUR SPECIFIC PROBLEM]

Cross-pollinate from these unexpected fields:
1. BIOLOGY: What does nature solve that's analogous to my problem? What would evolution's answer be?
2. MILITARY STRATEGY: What would Sun Tzu, Boyd's OODA loop, or modern asymmetric warfare teach me?
3. IMPROVISATION (JAZZ/COMEDY): What principles of "yes, and" or structured improvisation apply?
4. ARCHITECTURE: How would an architect approach the structural challenges in my problem?
5. GAME DESIGN: What would a game designer do to make this engaging and self-reinforcing?
6. COOKING: What would a chef's approach to ingredients, timing, and presentation teach me?

For each domain:
- The specific principle or technique that transfers
- How to adapt it to my situation
- Why this works (the underlying pattern)

Then identify the ONE cross-domain insight that could genuinely change my approach. Develop it fully.
