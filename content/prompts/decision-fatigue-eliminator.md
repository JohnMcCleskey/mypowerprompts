Decision fatigue is silently destroying your best work. Every small decision (what to eat, what to wear, what to work on next) drains the same mental battery you need for breakthroughs. Let's fix that.

My typical day involves these recurring decisions:
[LIST 5-10 DECISIONS YOU MAKE REPEATEDLY — meals, meetings, task order, email responses, etc.]

Create a complete decision pre-set system:
1. DEFAULT ANSWERS: For each recurring decision, give me a pre-loaded default that eliminates the choice
2. IF-THEN RULES: "If X happens, I always do Y" — build 10 rules that cover 80% of situations
3. THE 2-MINUTE RULE LIST: Decisions that should never take more than 2 minutes, with a timer protocol
4. THE BIG THREE: Identify the 3 decisions per day that actually deserve my full attention — everything else gets a default
5. THE EVENING PRE-LOAD: A 5-minute ritual that pre-decides tomorrow's choices tonight

The goal: I should arrive at my desk with zero decisions already made except the ones that matter.
