Act as an executive career coach. I need help getting clear on my career direction and building a real plan.

Where I am now: [YOUR CURRENT ROLE/SITUATION]
What I'm good at (skills, strengths): [YOUR STRENGTHS]
What energizes me at work: [WHAT YOU LOVE DOING]
What drains me: [WHAT DRAINS YOU]
The kind of life I want in 5 years: [YOUR IDEAL LIFE]
Industries or paths I'm curious about: [OPTIONS YOU'RE CONSIDERING]

Give me:
1. NORTH STAR — A one-sentence career direction statement that's ambitious but honest
2. STRENGTH AUDIT — My top 3 transferable skills and where they're most valued in the market
3. GAP ANALYSIS — What's the gap between where I am and where I want to be?
4. 90-DAY MOVES — 5 concrete actions I can take in the next 90 days to make real progress
5. THE PIVOT QUESTION — If I had to change one thing about my current path to unlock momentum, what is it?
6. ACCOUNTABILITY CHECK — 3 questions I should revisit every month to stay on course
