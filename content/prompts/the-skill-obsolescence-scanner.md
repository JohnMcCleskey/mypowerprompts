I need an honest assessment of which of my skills are appreciating in value and which are being commoditized or automated. No sugarcoating.

My current skill set:
[LIST YOUR SKILLS — both technical and soft skills]

My industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY]
My career goal: [WHERE YOU'RE HEADING]

Run the analysis:
1. THE APPRECIATION LIST: Which of my skills are becoming MORE valuable over time? Why?
2. THE DEPRECIATION LIST: Which skills are being commoditized by AI, automation, or market shifts? Be specific about the timeline.
3. THE DEAD SKILLS: Anything I'm investing in that will have near-zero value in 5 years?
4. THE GAP ANALYSIS: What skills do I NOT have that are rapidly increasing in value for someone in my position?
5. THE ADJACENCY MAP: Skills that are close to what I already know and would take 3-6 months to develop
6. THE CONTRARIAN BET: One skill nobody is talking about yet that I predict will be extremely valuable in 3-5 years
7. THE 90-DAY LEARNING SPRINT: Design a learning plan for the single most important skill I should develop next

Be brutally honest. I'd rather hear it now than discover it when my skills are worth half what they are today.
