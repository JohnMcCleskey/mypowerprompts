Give me a comprehensive strategic briefing on the current state of AI as if you're a senior analyst presenting to a board of directors. Focus on:

1. THE CAPABILITY FRONTIER: What can AI do now that seemed impossible 2 years ago? Give specific, concrete examples.
2. THE SURPRISE DEVELOPMENTS: What has emerged that experts didn't expect?
3. THE GAPS: What problem do people desperately want AI to solve that it still can't?
4. THE BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY MAP: Three industries being quietly transformed right now
5. THE RISK LANDSCAPE: What's the thing that could go wrong that we're not talking about enough?
6. THE 12-MONTH PREDICTION: One specific capability that will arrive in the next year
7. THE QUESTION NOBODY IS ASKING: What should we be thinking about that we're not?

Be specific. Avoid generalities. Treat me as someone who can handle nuance.
