Run a competitive intelligence analysis on my space as if you were a strategic analyst presenting to my board. I need to know who to worry about, who to watch, and who to ignore.

My business/project: [WHAT YOU DO]
My direct competitors: [WHO YOU COMPETE WITH]
My market: [YOUR MARKET/INDUSTRY]

The intelligence brief:
1. THE LANDSCAPE MAP: Categorize all competitors into: Direct Threats / Parallel Players / Potential Disruptors / Dead on Arrival
2. THE STRENGTH/WEAKNESS TABLE: For each direct threat, their biggest strength and biggest vulnerability
3. THE MOAT ANALYSIS: What's stopping competitors from copying my best features? What's stopping me from copying theirs?
4. THE BLIND SPOT: Who or what is NOT on my radar that should be? (this is always the most valuable part)
5. THE ASYMMETRIC OPPORTUNITIES: Where can I win that they can't follow? (usually involves being smaller, faster, or more specialized)
6. THE TIMELINE: What's going to change in this competitive landscape in the next 12 months?
7. THE THREE MOVES: Three specific strategic actions I should take based on this analysis

Be specific about companies/products when possible. Generic competitive analysis is useless.
