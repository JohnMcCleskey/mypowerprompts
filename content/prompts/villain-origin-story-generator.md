Every great supervillain has an origin story — usually something embarrassingly small that broke them. Take my worst day and write my villain origin story.

My terrible day: [DESCRIBE YOUR BAD DAY]

Write:
1. The origin story (dramatic, movie-trailer narration style)
2. My supervillain name and power (directly related to what went wrong)
3. My nemesis (the hero who represents what frustrated me)
4. My lair and henchmen theme
5. The ONE dramatic monologue I'd give when I finally take over

Make it both ridiculous and oddly cathartic.
