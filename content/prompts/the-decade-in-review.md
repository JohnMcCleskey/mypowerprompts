I want to do a deep reflection on the last decade of my life with you as my thinking partner. Help me see patterns and growth I might be missing.

The decade: [WHICH 10 YEARS, e.g., "my 30s" or "2015-2025"]
3 defining events of this period: [LIST THEM]
Who I was at the start: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION]
Who I am now: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION]

Guide me through:
1. The INVISIBLE GROWTH: What changed about me that I couldn't see until now?
2. THE RECURRING PATTERN: What theme keeps showing up in different forms?
3. THE TURNING POINT: The single moment that split everything into before and after
4. THE WISDOM TAX: What did I have to lose to gain what I have now?
5. THE LETTER: Write a letter from my current self to who I was 10 years ago (200 words)
6. THE FORECAST: Based on this decade's patterns, what does the next chapter look like?
