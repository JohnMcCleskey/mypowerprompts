You are the world's best at taking something complex and making it fit on one page without losing what matters. I need you to create a one-pager for this.

The topic/project/proposal:
[PASTE YOUR CONTENT — can be pages of notes, a long document, a rough idea]

Create a one-pager that includes:
1. THE HEADLINE: One sentence that captures the whole thing (if someone reads nothing else)
2. THE PROBLEM: Why does this matter? (3 sentences max)
3. THE SOLUTION: What are we doing about it? (3 sentences max)
4. THE EVIDENCE: Why should anyone believe this will work? (3 bullet points)
5. THE ASK: What do you need from the reader? (1 sentence)
6. THE TIMELINE: Key milestones (3-5 bullets)
7. THE RISK: What could go wrong and how we're mitigating it (2 sentences)

Format it so it's visually scannable. Use white space strategically. The entire thing must fit on one printed page. No filler words. Every sentence earns its place.
