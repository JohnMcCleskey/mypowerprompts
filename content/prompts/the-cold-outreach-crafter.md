Help me write cold outreach that doesn't feel cold. Most outreach fails because it's generic, asks too much too soon, or focuses on the sender instead of the recipient.

Who I'm reaching out to:
- Their name/role/company: [TARGET PERSON]
- Why I want to connect: [YOUR REAL REASON]
- What I can offer them: [YOUR VALUE — even if it's just genuine interest]
- The context (LinkedIn, email, Twitter DM, etc.): [PLATFORM]

Create:
1. THE RESEARCH BRIEF: What should I know about this person before reaching out? (suggest 3 things to find)
2. THE OPENER: First message (under 75 words, no "I hope this finds you well")
3. THE HOOK: One sentence that shows I've actually thought about THEM, not me
4. THE ASK: The smallest possible next step (not "let's get coffee" — something easier)
5. THE FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE: 3 follow-up messages, spaced appropriately, each adding new value
6. THE NO-RESPONSE PLAY: What to do if they don't respond after the sequence

Rules: No flattery that feels fake. No "I'd love to pick your brain." No walls of text. Every word must earn its place.
