I need to prepare for uncertainty. Instead of predicting one future, let's model multiple scenarios so I'm ready for whatever happens.

My situation: [YOUR CURRENT SITUATION]
The key uncertainty: [THE THING YOU CAN'T PREDICT]
Time horizon: [6 MONTHS / 1 YEAR / 3 YEARS]

Build 4 scenarios:
1. THE BEST CASE (Probability: ___): Everything goes right. What does this look like in vivid detail? What would I need to do to maximize this outcome?

2. THE BASE CASE (Probability: ___): Things continue roughly as they are. What's the trajectory? What slowly gets better/worse?

3. THE CHALLENGE CASE (Probability: ___): Something significant goes wrong. What is it? How bad does it get? What's my recovery plan?

4. THE WILDCARD (Probability: ___): Something nobody expects happens. What could it be? How would it change everything?

For each scenario:
- Early warning signals that this scenario is unfolding
- My optimal strategy for this world
- The one action I should take NOW that pays off across all scenarios

End with: THE ROBUST STRATEGY — the approach that works reasonably well in all 4 scenarios.
