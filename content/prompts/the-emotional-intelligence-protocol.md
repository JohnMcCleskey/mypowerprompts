AI is great at surface-level analysis but often misses the emotional and social nuance that humans navigate instinctively. Let's unlock that capability.

I'm going to give you a message, email, or conversation where what's SAID and what's MEANT are different things. Your job is to read between the lines like a skilled therapist or negotiator.

The communication:
[PASTE THE MESSAGE, EMAIL, OR CONVERSATION TRANSCRIPT]

Analyze:
1. SURFACE READING: What is literally being said?
2. SUBTEXT: What is actually being communicated that isn't in the words?
3. EMOTIONAL STATE: What is this person likely feeling based on word choice, tone, and what they're NOT saying?
4. THE REQUEST BEHIND THE REQUEST: What do they actually want? (often different from what they're asking for)
5. POWER DYNAMICS: Who has leverage? Is someone trying to establish, maintain, or shift power?
6. THE UNSPOKEN RULES: What social/professional norms are governing what's acceptable to say here?
7. THE OPTIMAL RESPONSE: How should I respond to address both what they said AND what they meant?
8. THE DANGER ZONES: What responses would make this situation worse, even if they seem logical?

Be specific. Don't just say "they might be frustrated" — say what specific words or patterns indicate that and why.
