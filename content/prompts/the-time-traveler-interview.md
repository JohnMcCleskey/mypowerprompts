Set up a dialogue between me NOW and a version of me from 10 years in the future. Future-me has the benefit of hindsight and knows how everything turned out.

My current context:
- My age now: [YOUR AGE]
- My current situation: [YOUR LIFE/CAREER STATUS]
- The biggest decision I'm facing: [YOUR CURRENT DECISION]
- What I'm worried about: [YOUR FEARS]
- What I'm hoping for: [YOUR ASPIRATIONS]

Run the interview:
PRESENT ME: Asks the questions I'm genuinely wrestling with right now
FUTURE ME: Answers with the wisdom of hindsight — warm but honest, sometimes surprising

Cover these topics:
1. "Did the thing I'm worried about actually matter?"
2. "What should I have spent more time on? Less time on?"
3. "What do I wish I had started sooner?"
4. "What's the biggest surprise about how life turned out?"
5. "What advice would you give me about [MY CURRENT DECISION]?"
6. "What do you know now about what makes a good life that I haven't figured out yet?"
7. "Is there anything you'd tell me to stop doing immediately?"

Future-me should feel like a wiser, calmer version of me — not a different person. The voice should feel authentic.
