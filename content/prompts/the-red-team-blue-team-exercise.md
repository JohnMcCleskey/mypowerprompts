Run a Red Team / Blue Team exercise on my plan to stress-test it before I commit.

My plan: [YOUR PLAN IN DETAIL]

RED TEAM INSTRUCTIONS (paste into first AI conversation):
You are a Red Team analyst. Your job is to find every way this plan could fail, be exploited, or produce unintended consequences. Be creative, thorough, and uncomfortable. Give your top 10 attack vectors ranked by likelihood × damage.

BLUE TEAM INSTRUCTIONS (paste into second AI conversation):
You are a Blue Team strategist defending this plan against attack. After the Red Team sends their report, respond to each attack with: (a) how likely it actually is, (b) the mitigation strategy, (c) residual risk.

HOW TO RUN IT:
1. Paste Red Team prompt + your plan → get their attack report
2. Paste Blue Team prompt + your plan + Red Team's attacks → get defense
3. Ask Red Team to review Blue Team's defenses and identify what's still vulnerable
4. Make final decisions based on what survives all three rounds
