I have multiple ideas competing for my limited time and energy. Instead of agonizing over which one to pursue, let's run a tournament where ideas compete head-to-head and the best one wins.

My ideas:
[LIST 4-8 IDEAS YOU'RE CONSIDERING — projects, business ideas, creative pursuits, etc.]

Run the tournament:
ROUND 1 — THE MATCHUPS: Randomly pair ideas and for each matchup:
- Compare them on: feasibility, potential impact, personal excitement, uniqueness, and time-to-first-result
- Declare a winner with reasoning
- Note: Even the losing idea's best quality gets carried forward

ROUND 2 — SEMIFINALS: Winners face off with deeper analysis:
- What does success look like for each at 6 months?
- Which has more asymmetric upside?
- Which one energizes me more (based on what I've shared)?

ROUND 3 — THE FINAL: The top 2 ideas go head to head:
- Make the strongest case for each
- Identify the deciding factor
- Declare the champion

POST-TOURNAMENT:
- THE CHAMPION: Why this idea won and the first three steps to start
- THE DARK HORSE: The eliminated idea that could have won under different criteria
- THE COMBINATION: Is there a way to merge any two ideas into something better than either alone?
