Apply the Pareto Principle to every area of my work and life with surgical precision. Most people understand 80/20 intellectually but never actually apply it. Let's change that.

My current situation:
- My work/business: [DESCRIBE WHAT YOU DO]
- My main goals: [YOUR TOP 3 GOALS]
- Where I spend most of my time: [YOUR TIME BREAKDOWN]
- My biggest sources of income/value: [WHERE VALUE COMES FROM]

Run the 80/20 audit:
1. REVENUE/VALUE: What 20% of my activities generate 80% of my results? Be specific.
2. TIME WASTERS: What 80% of my activities produce only 20% of value? Name them.
3. RELATIONSHIPS: Which 20% of my relationships drive 80% of my growth?
4. SKILLS: Which 20% of my skills are responsible for 80% of my advantage?
5. THE UNCOMFORTABLE CUT: List everything I should stop doing immediately
6. THE DOUBLING PLAN: If I spent 2x the time on my top 20%, what would happen?
7. THE 64/4 RULE: Apply 80/20 twice — find the 4% that drives 64% of everything

End with a concrete weekly schedule that reflects these findings.
