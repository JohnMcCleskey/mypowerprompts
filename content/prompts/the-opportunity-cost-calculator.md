Every yes is a no to something else. Most people never calculate what they're giving up. Let's make my tradeoffs visible.

I'm currently spending my time on:
[LIST YOUR MAJOR TIME COMMITMENTS AND ROUGHLY HOW MANY HOURS PER WEEK]

I'm considering:
[DESCRIBE A NEW OPPORTUNITY, PROJECT, OR COMMITMENT YOU'RE EVALUATING]

Calculate my opportunity costs:
1. THE TIME AUDIT: Where will the hours for this new thing actually come from? What gets squeezed?
2. THE TRUE COST: Beyond time — what energy, attention, and relationships does this cost?
3. THE HIDDEN YES: What am I unconsciously saying yes to by taking this on? (stress? growth? status?)
4. THE HIDDEN NO: What specific thing dies or gets neglected if I say yes?
5. THE 10/10/10 TEST: How will I feel about this decision in 10 minutes? 10 months? 10 years?
6. THE HELL YES TEST: Is this a "hell yes"? If not, it's a no.
7. THE REVERSAL TEST: If I was already doing this, would I be excited to stop?
8. THE FINAL VERDICT: Should I do this, and if so, what specifically must I eliminate to make room?

I want clarity, not permission. Help me see what I can't see right now.
