Act as a personal systems architect. I need a custom organization system built around how I actually think and work, not a generic productivity framework.

How I currently manage tasks/info: [WHAT YOU DO NOW — even if it's "chaos"]
Where things fall through the cracks most often: [YOUR PAIN POINTS]
Tools I already use and like: [YOUR CURRENT TOOLS]
My work style (planner vs. spontaneous, visual vs. text, etc.): [HOW YOU WORK BEST]
The 3 areas of my life most in need of organization: [WORK / FINANCE / HEALTH / PROJECTS / etc.]

Design my system:
1. CAPTURE LAYER — One frictionless method to capture everything that enters my world
2. WEEKLY REVIEW RITUAL — A 15-minute weekly review process I'll actually do
3. TOOL STACK — The exact tools and how they connect (keep it minimal — max 3 tools)
4. INBOX ZERO PROTOCOL — How I process email, messages, and requests without letting them pile up
5. PROJECT TRACKER — A simple template to track each active project's status, next action, and deadline
6. EMERGENCY RESET — A 30-minute "rescue protocol" for when everything feels out of control
