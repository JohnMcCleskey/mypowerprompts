Act as a strategic life coach. Help me audit whether what I'm doing every day is actually aligned with what I say I want.

My stated top 3 goals right now: [YOUR GOALS]
What I actually spend most of my time on: [HONEST BREAKDOWN OF YOUR WEEK]
What I say no to (or avoid): [WHAT YOU DODGE OR DELAY]
My biggest time sinks: [WHERE TIME DISAPPEARS]
How I feel about my progress right now (1-10 and why): [YOUR HONEST RATING]

Run the alignment audit:
1. ALIGNMENT SCORE — Rate how aligned my daily actions are with each of my 3 goals (0-10 per goal)
2. THE LIE I'M TELLING MYSELF — The single biggest rationalization I'm using to avoid doing the hard thing
3. TIME REALLOCATION — If I had to move 5 hours/week from what I'm doing to what matters, what gets cut and what gets added?
4. IDENTITY QUESTION — What kind of person would naturally achieve my goals, and how does my current behavior compare?
5. THE ONE DOMINO — If I changed just ONE behavior this month, which change would have the biggest ripple effect on all 3 goals?
6. 30-DAY REALIGNMENT PLAN — A concrete 4-week plan to close the gap between my actions and my goals
