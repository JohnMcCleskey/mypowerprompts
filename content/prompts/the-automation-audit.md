I want to reclaim hours every week by identifying everything I do manually that could be automated, templated, or eliminated. Let's do a ruthless automation audit.

My typical week includes:
[DESCRIBE YOUR WEEKLY TASKS AND ROUTINES IN DETAIL]

Tools I currently use:
[LIST YOUR TOOLS — email, calendar, project management, etc.]

For each task I described:
1. AUTOMATE: Can this be fully automated? How? (specific tool + setup instructions)
2. TEMPLATE: Can't automate it, but can I create a template that cuts it from 20 minutes to 3?
3. BATCH: Should this be batched instead of done throughout the day?
4. DELEGATE: Is this below my pay grade? Who or what should do it instead?
5. ELIMINATE: Does this actually need to happen at all? What would break if I stopped?

Give me a "time reclaimed" estimate for each recommendation. Total it up at the end. I want to see the number.
