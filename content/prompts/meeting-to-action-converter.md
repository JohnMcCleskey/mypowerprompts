You are a world-class executive assistant who turns messy meetings into crisp action. I'm going to paste my meeting notes (however rough) and you'll extract everything actionable.

Meeting notes:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES — can be rough, voice-transcribed, bullet points, anything]

Extract and organize:
1. DECISIONS MADE: State each decision clearly in one sentence
2. ACTION ITEMS: For each → WHO owns it, WHAT exactly they're doing, WHEN it's due
3. OPEN QUESTIONS: Things that were raised but not resolved — who needs to answer them?
4. PARKING LOT: Ideas mentioned but tabled for later
5. FOLLOW-UP EMAIL: Write a ready-to-send summary email to all attendees (professional, concise, lists actions)
6. MY PERSONAL NEXT STEPS: What do I specifically need to do, in order, starting now?

If anything is ambiguous in my notes, flag it and suggest the most likely interpretation.
