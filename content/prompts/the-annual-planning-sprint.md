Let's plan my year — not with vague resolutions, but with a framework that actually works. This should feel like a strategy session, not a wish list.

My starting point:
- Where I am right now (professionally): [YOUR CURRENT STATE]
- Where I am right now (personally): [YOUR LIFE STATE]
- My single biggest ambition: [YOUR NORTH STAR]
- What I'm most afraid won't change: [YOUR FEAR]

Build my annual plan:
1. THE 3 BETS: What are the 3 big bets I'm making this year? (things with asymmetric upside)
2. THE ANTI-GOALS: What do I NOT want my year to look like? (sometimes clearer than goals)
3. QUARTERLY MILESTONES: Break the year into 4 chapters, each with one defining milestone
4. THE KEYSTONE HABIT: One daily habit that would make everything else easier
5. THE SKILL INVESTMENT: One skill to develop this year that pays dividends for 10 years
6. THE RELATIONSHIP INVESTMENT: One relationship to deepen that could change my trajectory
7. THE QUIT LIST: Things I'm going to stop doing to make room for what matters
8. THE 12-MONTH SCORECARD: 3-5 metrics I'll track to know if the year was successful

Design it so I can review this plan every Sunday in 5 minutes.
