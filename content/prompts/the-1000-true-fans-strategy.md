I want to find my true niche — specific enough to matter, but compelling enough to grow. Help me find my 1,000 true fans.

What I do/create/offer: [YOUR WORK]
Who I think my audience is: [GENERAL DESCRIPTION]
What makes me different: [YOUR ANSWER - even if vague]

Help me:
1. Find 10 hyper-specific niches within my broader audience
2. Pick the most promising one and describe them in intimate detail (their daily frustrations, dreams, vocabulary, watering holes)
3. What would I create ONLY for them that nobody else would?
4. What is the "insider language" that would make them feel immediately seen?
5. How do I reach the first 100 before the first 1,000?
6. What does "winning" look like in year 1 with this audience?
