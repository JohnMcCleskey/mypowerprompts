Transport me into a historical moment so vividly that I can feel what it was like to actually live it.

Historical period or event: [THE ERA OR EVENT]
Perspective: [WHOSE EYES DO YOU WANT TO SEE THROUGH?]

Create:
1. A "day in the life" account from that perspective (400 words, present tense, first person)
2. What this person knew, believed, feared, and hoped for
3. What they could NOT know that we know now (dramatic irony)
4. Three decisions they faced that had no obvious right answer
5. What connects their experience to something in our world today
6. The one question you'd ask them if you had five minutes

Make it feel like time travel, not a history lesson.
