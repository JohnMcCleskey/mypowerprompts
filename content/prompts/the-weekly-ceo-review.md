Run me through a CEO-level weekly review. This isn't a to-do list check — this is strategic reflection that separates people who are busy from people who are effective.

My week:
- What I planned to accomplish: [YOUR PLAN]
- What I actually accomplished: [REALITY]
- Biggest win: [YOUR WIN]
- Biggest frustration: [YOUR FRUSTRATION]
- Hours worked: [APPROXIMATE]

Execute this review:
1. THE HONESTY CHECK: Where did I confuse motion with progress? What felt productive but wasn't?
2. THE LEVERAGE AUDIT: Which 2 hours this week produced the most value? Why?
3. THE ENERGY MAP: When was I in flow? When was I forcing it? What's the pattern?
4. THE RELATIONSHIP PULSE: Did I invest in the right people this week? Who did I neglect?
5. THE NEXT WEEK DESIGN: Based on this week's data, design next week to be 30% more effective
6. THE ONE THING: If next week could only accomplish ONE outcome, what should it be?

Be brutally honest. I need a mirror, not a cheerleader.
