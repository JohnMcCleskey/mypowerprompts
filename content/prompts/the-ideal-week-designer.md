Help me design my ideal week — not a fantasy, but a realistic template that matches how my brain and body actually work.

My inputs:
- Work hours: [YOUR TYPICAL WORK HOURS]
- Non-negotiable commitments: [MEETINGS, FAMILY, EXERCISE, ETC.]
- My peak energy time: [MORNING/AFTERNOON/EVENING]
- My worst energy time: [WHEN DO YOU CRASH?]
- My most important recurring tasks: [LIST THEM]
- What I wish I had more time for: [WHAT'S GETTING SQUEEZED OUT]

Design my week:
1. ENERGY-MATCHED SCHEDULING: Place tasks when my brain is best equipped for them
2. THEME DAYS: Assign cognitive themes (creative, administrative, strategic, social) to reduce context switching
3. BUFFER ZONES: Build in transition time between modes (most people don't)
4. THE SACRED BLOCK: One 3-hour window per week that is untouchable for deep work
5. THE RECOVERY PROTOCOL: When and how to recharge without guilt
6. THE FLEX SLOT: Where does unexpected work go without destroying the system?

Present it as a visual weekly grid with time blocks, then explain the reasoning behind each choice.
