Everyone has a "personal monopoly" — a unique combination of skills, experiences, and personality that makes them the only person in the world who can do a specific thing. Most people never identify theirs. Let's find yours.

My ingredients:
- My professional skills: [LIST THEM]
- My unusual hobbies or interests: [THE WEIRD ONES COUNT MOST]
- My life experiences that shaped me: [UNUSUAL OR FORMATIVE EXPERIENCES]
- What people come to me for advice on: [WHAT DO PEOPLE ASK YOU?]
- What I'm passionate about that most people in my field aren't: [YOUR UNIQUE PASSION]
- My personality traits: [HOW WOULD FRIENDS DESCRIBE YOU?]

Build my personal monopoly:
1. THE COMBINATION ANALYSIS: What 3-ingredient combination is unique to me?
2. THE POSITIONING STATEMENT: One sentence that describes my unique value in a way that makes people say "I need exactly that"
3. THE PROOF POINTS: 3 examples from my past that prove this positioning is authentic
4. THE AUDIENCE: Who specifically would value this unique combination most?
5. THE COMPETITION CHECK: Who else is close to this positioning? What makes me different?
6. THE AMPLIFICATION PLAN: How do I make this unique positioning more visible and more extreme?

The goal: I should be the obvious choice for a very specific type of problem.
