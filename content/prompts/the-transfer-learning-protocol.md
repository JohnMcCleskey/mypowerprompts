I had a breakthrough in one area using AI, and I want to transfer that insight to a completely different domain. The ability to transfer learning across contexts is a meta-skill that multiplies everything.

My breakthrough:
- Domain where it happened: [FIRST DOMAIN]
- The insight or technique that worked: [WHAT I DISCOVERED]
- Why it worked: [YOUR THEORY]

Domain I want to apply it to: [COMPLETELY DIFFERENT DOMAIN]

Execute the transfer:
1. ABSTRACTION: Strip the insight down to its underlying principle (domain-independent)
2. ANALOGY MAP: Find the structural parallels between the two domains
3. ADAPTATION: How does the principle need to change to work in the new domain?
4. APPLICATION: Apply the transferred insight to a specific challenge in the new domain
5. VALIDATION: What would prove this transfer actually works? Design a quick test.
6. EXTENSION: What OTHER domains could this principle transfer to? (aim for 3 more)

Then teach me THE META-SKILL: A repeatable process I can use whenever I have a breakthrough in any area to systematically check if it applies elsewhere. Most people have great insights and use them in exactly one context. This protocol prevents that.
