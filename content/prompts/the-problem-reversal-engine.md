Instead of solving my problem directly, let's solve it by inversion — figuring out how to make the problem WORSE, then reversing those insights into solutions.

My problem: [DESCRIBE YOUR PROBLEM]

Execute the reversal:
1. THE ANTI-GOAL: How would I GUARANTEE this problem gets worse? List 10 specific actions.
2. THE INVERSION: For each action, flip it into its opposite — these are your solution candidates.
3. THE HIDDEN INSIGHT: Which inverted solutions reveal something about the problem I hadn't considered?
4. THE SABOTEUR'S PLAYBOOK: If I were secretly trying to prevent this problem from ever being solved, what would I do? (This reveals hidden blockers)
5. THE NAIVE QUESTION: What would someone with zero context ask about this problem that an insider would never think to ask?
6. THE CONSTRAINT REMOVAL: If I could magically remove one constraint, which removal would make the problem trivially easy? (This identifies the real bottleneck)
7. THE SYNTHESIS: Combine the 3 most surprising insights into an unconventional solution approach

The best answer is usually hiding in the place you haven't looked.
