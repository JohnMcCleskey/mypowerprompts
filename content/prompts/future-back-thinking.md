Let's do future-back thinking. I want to design the future first, then figure out how to get there.

Domain: [YOUR FIELD OR AREA OF INTEREST]
Your ambitious 10-year vision: [WHAT DOES REMARKABLE SUCCESS LOOK LIKE?]

Work backward:
- Year 10: Describe the future state in vivid, specific detail
- Year 7: What major milestone proves we're on track?
- Year 5: The inflection point that made everything possible
- Year 3: The unexpected breakthrough nobody saw coming
- Year 1: The foundation that had to be built first
- Month 3: The single experiment that validated the whole direction
- Week 1: The conversation or action that set this in motion

Now identify: What assumption about the future are we making, and what would change if that assumption is wrong?
