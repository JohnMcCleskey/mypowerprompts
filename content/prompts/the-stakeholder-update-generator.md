Write a polished status update that makes complex work look organized and builds confidence in my leadership. This should take my messy reality and turn it into executive-ready communication.

My raw update:
- What I/we've been working on: [DESCRIBE YOUR RECENT WORK]
- What's going well: [WINS]
- What's blocked or at risk: [PROBLEMS]
- What I need from leadership/stakeholders: [ASKS]
- Upcoming milestones: [WHAT'S NEXT]

Generate three versions:
1. THE SLACK UPDATE: Casual but professional, 4-5 bullet points, uses emoji tastefully
2. THE EMAIL UPDATE: Structured with headers, suitable for a VP or C-suite audience
3. THE BOARD SLIDE: Content for a single presentation slide — headline, 3 bullets, one metric

For each version:
- Lead with impact, not activity (what changed, not what I did)
- Frame risks as "items I'm managing" not "problems I have"
- Include one forward-looking statement that shows strategic thinking
- End with a clear next step
