## Concept

Archimedes said "give me a lever long enough and a place to stand, and I'll move the world." Leverage is any mechanism that multiplies force — in modern life, it's code, media, capital, and people. Naval Ravikant modernized this: the most powerful leverage is permissionless — you don't need anyone's approval to write code that scales to millions or publish content that spreads globally. The key question stops being "how hard am I working?" and becomes "how much leverage am I operating with?" A person with high leverage creates orders of magnitude more output than one without, for the same hours of input.

## Key insight

You can't outwork leverage. A person writing code or creating media can reach a million people for zero marginal cost. A person giving advice one-on-one can reach one. Same hours. Radically different output. The goal is to find the lever, not to push harder.

## Practice prompt

Help me find and maximize my leverage — identifying where I have disproportionate force that I'm currently underusing.

My skills, knowledge, and resources: [WHAT YOU'RE GOOD AT, WHAT YOU KNOW, WHAT YOU HAVE ACCESS TO]
My current work or project: [WHAT YOU'RE WORKING ON]
My goal: [WHAT OUTCOME YOU WANT TO CREATE]

Map my leverage across all four modern types:

LABOR LEVERAGE — Who could I recruit or partner with who is better than me at parts of this? What could I delegate that's below my highest-value use of time?

CAPITAL LEVERAGE — How could capital (money, tools, paid services) replace my time? What's the 10x ROI investment I'm not making?

CODE/PRODUCT LEVERAGE — What could I build once that works while I sleep? What repetitive thing I'm doing manually could be automated?

MEDIA/CONTENT LEVERAGE — What knowledge do I have that could be packaged once and distributed to thousands? What would a blog post, video, or tool create that a conversation couldn't?

Now: Where am I operating with the least leverage right now? What's the specific thing I should stop doing manually, and what should replace it?

Make my effort stop being linearly correlated with my output.

## A small challenge

Identify one thing you do repeatedly that could be automated, templated, or turned into content. That's your leverage point. What's the first step to build it?
