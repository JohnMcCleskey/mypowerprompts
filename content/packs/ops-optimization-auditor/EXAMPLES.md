# Examples

## Example 1
Input: "Audit our onboarding workflow and find the biggest friction points."

Output:
- Ranked bottlenecks in the current onboarding flow
- Evidence-backed observations
- 3-5 prioritized improvements
- Suggested owner and validation metric for each

## Example 2
Input: "We keep missing internal deadlines. What should we optimize?"

Output:
- Diagnosis of the scheduling, handoff, and review failure points
- Root-cause hypotheses
- Recommendations sorted by impact and difficulty
