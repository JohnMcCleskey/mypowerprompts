# Handoffs

Use this pack when you need an audit that can be handed to an operator, manager, or implementation team.

## Handoff Requirements
- State the problem clearly.
- Include the evidence used.
- Separate quick wins from structural fixes.
- Call out dependencies, approvals, and sequencing.

## When Escalating
- Escalate when the recommendation depends on policy, budget, access, or cross-team ownership.
- Escalate when the cost of delay is high or the data is too weak to recommend a change confidently.
