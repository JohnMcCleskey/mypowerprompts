# Memory

## Durable Memory Categories
- Target domain and use case.
- Preferred level of maturity and risk tolerance.
- Providers or stacks already in play.
- Stable portability requirements.

## Sensitive Data Restrictions
- Do not retain credentials, private roadmaps, or sensitive internal strategy unless necessary for the task.
- Keep source-specific details minimal when they are not needed later.

## What Can Be Summarized
- Capability categories.
- Provider comparisons.
- Maturity notes.
- Decision recommendations and why they were chosen.

## Reset State When
- The target domain changes.
- Freshness matters again and the previous source set is stale.
- The user switches providers, time horizon, or risk tolerance.
