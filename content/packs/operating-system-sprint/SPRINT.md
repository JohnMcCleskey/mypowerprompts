# 5-Day Sprint

## Day 1: Inventory
Map recurring work, tools, files, handoffs, decisions, and pain points.

## Day 2: Bottlenecks
Find delays, rework, missed context, manual copy/paste, and quality drift.

## Day 3: Loop Design
Choose loops with trigger, context, action, review, memory, approval.

## Day 4: Install Routines
Draft the prompt/skill/agent/workflow package and run a low-risk test.

## Day 5: Review and Adopt
Evaluate outcomes, approve standing routines, and schedule the next review.
