# Inputs

## Preferred Inputs
- List of systems, repositories, docs, folders, dashboards, or tools to inspect
- Business or team goals
- Known pain points
- Recent work examples
- Quality standards or acceptance criteria
- Current constraints, permissions, and approval boundaries

## Optional Inputs
- Metrics or KPIs
- Customer feedback
- Support logs or issue themes
- SOPs and handoff docs
- Roadmap or project board
- Calendar/process cadence
- Prior audits or retrospectives

## Minimum Useful Input
If the user cannot provide broad access, ask for:
- one workflow,
- one recent output,
- one pain point,
- one desired quality outcome.
