title: The Real Deal Check
slug: real-deal-check
tags: [shopping, personal finance, decisions]
what_it_does: Has your AI look up an item's recent price history and what other stores charge today, with links you can check, so you can tell a real Black Friday discount from a marked-up list price before you buy.
prompt: |
  Find this item's lowest price in the last 90 days and its price at other stores today, with links. Then say buy, wait, or skip: [link]
setup: Use an assistant with web search turned on (ChatGPT search, Claude with web search, Gemini, or Grok) and replace [link] with the product page or the exact model name and number. For Amazon items, it helps to also open the item on a price tracker such as Keepa or CamelCamelCamel and paste or screenshot the chart. Business owners can run the same prompt on software, equipment, or supply deals before buying.
check_it: Every price it reports should come with a link you can open. If it cannot find a price history, it should say so plainly rather than guess, and the buy, wait, or skip call should follow from the numbers it showed you. Open one or two links to confirm before you check out.
