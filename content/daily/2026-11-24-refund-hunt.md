what_it_does: Has your AI comb through order histories or card statements to find returns that were never refunded and charges that were duplicated or forgotten, then gives you a ready note to send each company's support team.
prompt: |
  Find every return I never got refunded for and every duplicate or forgotten charge. For each, list the date, amount, and a note for support.
setup: "Works in any assistant that accepts file uploads (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok). Export your order history (Amazon offers a \"Request Your Data\" privacy request, or you can copy your Your Orders and returns pages) or download card or bank statements as CSV or PDF, and upload them with the prompt. Remove full account numbers first. Business owners can run the same prompt on vendor invoices and the company card statement."
check_it: Spot-check two or three flagged items against the original order or statement before contacting anyone. The AI should show each item's date and amount so you can verify it, and it drafts the note but never sends it.
